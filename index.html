The British and American Word Families Project: A Morphological Database

Morphology, roots, affixes, compounds, frequency, and word-family structure

About this Database

  • Explore English word families, compound words, basewords, roots, prefixes, suffixes, and frequency measures.
  • The BAWF database is designed to support vocabulary, morphology, and word-family instruction.
  • Morphology is the study of how words are built from meaningful units, including bases, roots, prefixes, and suffixes.
  • Use the curated tab to focus on familiar and high-utility word families for teaching.
  • Use the full database tabs to explore the broader lexicon attested in large English corpora.
  • Use sorting, filtering, AI explanation prompts, dictionary links, etymology links, and AI parse review.
  • Report possible database errors or disagreements back to the project using the report tools.
  • Download the raw compressed CSV files for offline use and advanced analysis.

Be inspired by joyful word learning and reading — dive in and explore!

Note: Large datasets may need 30–60 seconds to load.

Column Definitions

Core Word Information

  • Word: The surface wordform.
  • Word_frq_Zipf / Word_frq_Zipf_Recomputed: Zipf frequency score of the wordform.
  • FLemma: Lemma or dictionary-style headword when available.
  • GPT_Coded_Word_Parts: GPT-assisted morphological breakdown of the word.

Baseword Information

  • Baseword: The central baseword of a word family.
  • Baseword_frq_Zipf: Word-family frequency computed from family members.
  • Baseword_Family_Size: Number of known wordforms in the baseword family.

Roots, Prefixes, and Suffixes

  • GPT_Root: Main root identified by GPT-assisted morphological analysis.
  • Greek_Latin_Root_1: First identified classical root when available.
  • Prefix_1 to Prefix_3: Prefixes identified in the word.
  • Suffix_1 to Suffix_6: Suffixes identified in the word.

Educational Measures

  • N_Morphemes_from_GPT_parse_without_inflections: Morpheme count excluding inflectional morphemes.
  • N_Morphemes_from_GPT_parse_plus_inflections: Morpheme count including inflectional morphemes.
  • Reading AoA Green et al._ 2025: Estimated reading age of acquisition.
  • Vocabulary eXpected Grade Level Flor et al._ 2024: Estimated vocabulary grade level.
  • GPT_Familiarity Brysbaert et al._ 2024: GPT-estimated familiarity rating when available.
